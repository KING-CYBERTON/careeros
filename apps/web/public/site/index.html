Questions

The ones job seekers ask before they sign up.

Send a question

How much does CareerOS cost for one person?

The Individual plan is under $29 a month. You can pay by card or M-Pesa.

Does the assistant rewrite my resume by itself?

No. It proposes each change, and you accept or reject it one by one.

Where do the matching jobs come from?

Scheduled searches read public job boards and put the matching roles in a review queue.

Can I take my resumes with me?

Yes. Resumes export to PDF, Word and JSON at any time.

How many resume templates are there?

There are 18 templates, and you can switch between them without retyping your resume.

Can my university or bootcamp use CareerOS?

Yes. The page for organisations covers the admin console and the three organisation plans.
