Questions

The ones organisations ask before they sign up.

Send a question

Is our data kept apart on the platform?

Yes. Each organisation's data is separated at the database level, and that separation is tested with every release.

How do we pay for a plan?

By card or M-Pesa, in US dollars or Kenyan shillings. Pro is billed per seat each month.

Is AI use limited on each plan?

Yes. Each plan includes an AI quota, and usage is metered per organisation.

What can admins see about applications?

Counts of applications, interviews and offers across the group, with a CSV export for reports.

What do members get on the platform?

The resume editor, the application tracker and the AI assistant on every plan. Job discovery comes with Pro and Enterprise.

Can members take their resumes with them?

Yes. Resumes export to PDF, Word and JSON at any time.
