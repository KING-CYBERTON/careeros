title: "Write emails to employers"

---

Write to a recruiter or hiring manager without starting from a blank page. **Write email** offers a short template for the stage your application is in, fills in the details it knows, and lets you change every word before you send it.

CareerOS never sends the email. You send it from your own mail app, or copy it and paste it wherever you write.

## Write an email from an application

1. Open the application and choose **Write email**. On the web it is on the **Messages** tab and beside the application's details. On mobile it is at the top of every workspace tab and on the application's **Details** screen.
2. Check the **Template**. The list starts with the templates that suit the stage: for an application at **Interview**, a thank-you. To see every template, select **Show templates for every stage**.
3. Read the **Subject** and **Message**. The company, role and a contact's name come from the application: the first contact with an email address, or the first contact if none has one. The interview date is the next interview on the application, or the most recent one if all have passed.
4. Edit either field. Your changes stay while you stay on that template; choosing another template starts again from its text.
5. Choose **Open in mail app**, or **Copy**.

### Fill in the [bracketed] parts

When the application does not hold a detail, the template shows its name in square brackets, such as `[interviewDate]` or `[contactName]`. A warning reminds you to replace them before you send. Add the contact's name and email address on the application to have them filled in next time.

### Open in mail app, or copy

**Open in mail app** opens a new message in your mail app with the contact's address, subject and text already in it. If the contact has no email address, the message opens without a recipient.

Some mail apps cannot open a link as long as a very long email. When the email is too long, **Open in mail app** copies it instead and tells you so. **Copy** always puts the subject, a blank line and the message on your clipboard.

## The variables

A template is plain text with variables in double braces. When it is filled in, each variable is replaced with the matching detail from your application and profile.

| Variable            | Replaced with                         |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| `{{firstName}}`     | Your first name                       |
| `{{fullName}}`      | Your name                             |
| `{{company}}`       | The company                           |
| `{{role}}`          | The role                              |
| `{{contactName}}`   | The contact's name on the application |
| `{{interviewDate}}` | The next interview's date             |
| `{{myEmail}}`       | Your email address                    |

A variable with no detail becomes a `[bracketed]` placeholder. A word in braces that is not on this list is left exactly as you typed it. Templates are text only: no formatting, links or code are run.

## Which template is offered

When several templates suit the stage, the list puts templates made for that stage first. Among those, yours come first, then your organization's, then the built-in ones, and **Write email** opens on the first.

An organization template replaces the built-in template of the same kind. Until an owner or admin sets one, members see the built-in. A template with no stage chosen suits every stage.

There are four built-in templates: **Follow up after applying**, **Thank you after an interview**, **Reach out to someone at the company** and **Respond to an offer**. They are written for CareerOS and are available in your language.

## Keep your own templates

Choose **Save as my template** in **Write email** to keep the text as it reads now. It is saved as written, with the company and names already in it; to reuse it for other applications, put the variables back where the details go.

To manage your templates, open **Settings → Email templates**. **New template** starts one from scratch, and **Edit** and **Archive** change your own. Templates from your organization and the built-in ones are listed below yours and cannot be changed there. Archiving only removes a template from your list. Emails you already sent are not affected.

Your templates are private to you. Other members of your organization, including its owners and admins, cannot see them.

## For owners and admins: set the organization's templates

Owners and admins can replace a built-in template for everyone in the organization. A member's own template of the same kind still comes first.

1. Open **Settings → Org email templates**.
2. On a built-in template, choose **Customise**. The editor starts from the built-in text.
3. Change the name, subject or message, and choose **Save template**.
4. To go back to the built-in text, choose **Archive** on the organization's template. Members see the built-in template again.

Each change is recorded in the organization's audit log, with the template's id and what kind of change it was, and never its text. Editing the organization's templates is available on the web. The mobile app shows them read-only, in **Settings → Email templates**, and points owners and admins to the web app.

## Limits

- The subject can be up to 300 characters, and the message up to 10,000.
- A personal workspace has no organization templates, because it has one member.
- Nothing is sent, tracked or opened from CareerOS. It does not know whether, or when, you sent the email.
