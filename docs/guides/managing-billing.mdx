**Billing** is where the owner of an organization pays for a plan. Owners and admins can see the plan, seats, status, AI credits, billing details and invoices. Only the owner can upgrade, change seats, update the card, change the billing details or cancel. The screenshots use a fictional organization and test payments.

Open it from **Settings → Billing**, or go to `/dashboard/organization/billing`.

## Read your plan

**Your plan** shows:

- The plan name and its status: **Trial** (with the days left), **Active**, **Ends at period end** (canceled, still working until the paid period ends), **Past due** (a payment failed, with the date by which to pay) or **Suspended** (members can view and export but not change data).
- **Seats**: the people in the organization against the seats you pay for.
- **Current period** and **Payment method**, when the payment provider has told us.
- **AI credits**: used and left this period. The credits are pooled across everyone in the organization.

The **Plans** list shows each plan with its monthly and annual price in Kenyan shillings and US dollars, exactly as the platform has set them. A plan marked **Contact us** is bought by arrangement.

## Upgrade or change plan

1. In **Plans**, choose **Choose** on a plan. The current plan offers **Change seats or interval**.
2. Pick **Monthly** or **Annual**.
3. Enter the number of **Seats**. It cannot be lower than the people already in the organization, and the page tells you so: remove members first. A plan can also have a seat limit.
4. Where the payment method is offered, choose **Card** (renews automatically) or **M-Pesa** (pays one period at a time, so you pay again when the period ends).
5. Choose **Continue to payment**. You go to the payment provider.

When you come back, the page says **Payment received, updating your plan** and checks every few seconds, for up to a minute, until the plan changes. If it takes longer, it tells you that confirmation may take a few minutes. You can leave the page: the plan updates as soon as the provider confirms. A payment that was declined changes nothing.

## Change seats, update your card or cancel

Once the organization pays, **Manage** appears for the owner:

- **Update seats** asks the provider to change the seat count. It cannot go below the number of members.
- **Update card or cancel** opens the payment provider's own page, when it has one.
- **Cancel subscription** asks you to confirm. The organization keeps working until the end of the period you already paid for, and nothing is charged after that.

Changes show once the provider confirms them.

## Billing details

**Billing details** holds what is printed in the "Bill to" block of every invoice: a **Legal name**, an **Address** (one line per line), a **KRA PIN** and a **Billing email**. The note under the title says what it is for: "Printed on your invoices."

- The owner edits the fields and chooses **Save billing details**. An empty field is left off the invoice. The billing email must be one plain address.
- An admin sees the details but cannot change them.
- Until the owner saves a legal name, an invoice shows the organization's name.
- The details are printed on every invoice, earlier ones included: an invoice shows the details that are current when you open it. Changing the details does not change the invoice number, the amounts or the date.

## Invoices

**Invoices** lists each payment with its **Invoice no.**, date, amount, method and a **Paid** badge. Choose **View** to open the invoice.

An invoice number looks like `INV-7F3K2Q-0003`: a prefix, a short code for your organization, and a counter that goes up by one with each payment. A number never changes once it has been issued.

The invoice page shows an A4 sheet:

- The platform's name and the seller's details (name, address, email, phone and tax PIN, when the operator has set them) and, on the right, the invoice title, number, issue date, paid date and the **PAID** badge.
- **Bill to** (your billing details) and **Payment** (method, reference and the period paid for).
- The line items with quantity, unit price and amount, then the **Subtotal**, the tax row, the **Total**, the **Amount paid** and the **Amount due**, which is zero because only completed payments are listed.

When the operator charges VAT, the sheet is titled **Tax invoice** and shows a row such as "VAT 16%", which is the part of the total that is tax. Otherwise it is titled **Invoice** and has no tax row. The amount you paid is the total.

Above the sheet:

- **Download invoice** saves the invoice as a PDF named after the invoice number, for example `INV-7F3K2Q-0003.pdf`. It is a single A4 page for one line item.
- **Print** prints the sheet on A4 paper with 18 mm margins and nothing else from the page.

An invoice holds no card data. It is a commercial invoice and receipt for your payment; a tax invoice issued through the Kenya Revenue Authority's eTIMS system, with its control unit number and QR code, is not part of it yet.

## Notices

- The owner sees a notice across the app when the trial ends in three days or fewer, when a payment is past due (with the date by which to pay) and when the organization is suspended, each with a link to Billing.
- Members see a short notice to contact the owner when a payment is overdue.

## When billing is off

On a server where billing is switched off, the page shows the plan read-only and says that billing is managed by the platform. There is nothing to pay.

## On the phone

The mobile app has **Settings → Plan**, a read-only view of the plan, status, seats and AI quota for every member. It shows no prices, invoices or billing details and has no way to buy, upgrade or cancel, because app store rules restrict selling subscriptions outside in-app purchase. The owner manages billing, billing details and invoices on the web.
